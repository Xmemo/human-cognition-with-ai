# Cognitive Augmentation & Governance

**Question:** How should human–AI cognitive systems be designed so that they improve capability without making agency, authorship, and responsibility opaque?

This domain focuses on interventions and system design. The central distinction is between AI that merely improves the current output and AI that changes the cognitive process in ways that support durable human capability.

## Radars

4. **Tools for Thought & Cognitive Augmentation** — process-oriented support, scaffolding, reflective interfaces, Socratic interaction, and systems that reveal rather than bypass cognitive gaps.
5. **Human–AI Collaboration & Hybrid Intelligence** — complementarity, task allocation, human–AI teaming, and conditions under which combinations outperform or underperform the best individual component.
6. **Cognitive Resilience, Authorship & Responsibility** — long-term cognitive sustainability, authorship/ownership, explainability-to-self, accountability, and non-delegable judgment.

## Design questions

- Which cognitive operations should AI automate, scaffold, or leave deliberately effortful?
- Does the interface preserve error detection, retrieval, comparison, and revision loops?
- Can the person explain, challenge, repair, or transfer the output after AI assistance?
- Who remains answerable for consequential claims and decisions?
