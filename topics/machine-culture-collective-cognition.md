# Machine Culture & Collective Cognition

**Question:** What happens when AI-mediated cognitive change propagates beyond individuals into groups, networks, norms, language, and cumulative culture?

This is the observatory's frontier layer. The unit of analysis changes from a person or human–AI pair to populations and cultural systems.

## Radars

7. **Machine Culture & Cultural Evolution** — AI as a source of cultural variation; machine discoveries; cultural transmission, retention, selection, and cumulative evolution.
8. **Collective Intelligence & Human–AI Groups** — group problem solving, collective innovation, coordination, information routing, and human–AI group performance.
9. **Social Learning, Norms & Cultural Feedback** — language change, norm formation, algorithmic mediation, and feedback loops such as `human → AI → human → culture → future AI`.

## Why this layer is distinct

A workflow can be individually rational yet collectively costly. For example, widespread substitution can raise short-term average performance while potentially compressing variation that cultural exploration depends on. Conversely, machine discoveries can sometimes enter human culture and become durable human knowledge. These are population-level questions and cannot be inferred from individual productivity alone.

## Frontier anchor

Brinkmann et al. (2026), **Propagation and preservation of AI-discovered problem-solving strategies in human culture**, experimentally identify non-triviality, learnability, and selective advantage as conditions under which machine-discovered strategies can persist in human populations. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-026-76113-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-76113-2).
