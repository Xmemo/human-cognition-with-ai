# Human Cognitive Change

**Question:** What happens to the human cognitive system as AI becomes part of ordinary thinking, learning, writing, and professional work?

This domain focuses on *effects and mechanisms*: what changes in human capability, monitoring, judgment, memory, creativity, writing processes, and expertise. It deliberately distinguishes short-term assisted performance from learning, transfer, and retained capability.

## Radars

1. **Cognitive Offloading, Skill Formation & Expertise** — what gets delegated; what is retained; whether skill gaps shrink, widen, or change form.
2. **Metacognition, Epistemic Agency & Judgment** — calibration, overreliance, verification, confidence, autonomy, and who retains the right to judge.
3. **Critical Thinking, Memory, Creativity & Writing Cognition** — measured changes in higher-order cognition and writing-as-thinking.

## Key distinctions

- output quality ≠ learning;
- AI-assisted performance ≠ independent performance;
- deeper AI use ≠ deeper cognition;
- self-reported competence ≠ measured competence;
- expertise is treated as a context tag and mechanism, not as a separate silo.
