## Integrity notes

- Citation counts supplied by discovery systems are intentionally omitted here because they change over time and do not determine evidence grade.
- Protocol papers are not treated as completed outcome studies.
- Conceptual papers are retained for mechanism/theory but not presented as causal evidence.
- `candidate/reference-only` means the item is preserved for traceability while excluded from the foundational set unless later verification upgrades its relevance or source confidence.
