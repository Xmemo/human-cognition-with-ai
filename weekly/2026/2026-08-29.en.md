## 1｜NEW — Layer-sensitive cognitive offloading in generative AI-assisted writing

**Source:** *Frontiers in Psychology* · **2026-08-28** · 8-week quasi-experiment  
**Radars:** `R1 R2 R3 R4` · **Evidence: [B]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1906199

Among 180 Chinese undergraduates across six intact classes, open AI collaboration produced the best AI-assisted writing performance. Yet on a supervised no-AI near-transfer task, bounded AI support with compulsory reflection outperformed open collaboration on writing quality, higher-order thinking, argument depth, and independent revision. The bundled intervention and small number of classes limit causal decomposition.

**Why it matters:** supported performance and post-AI residual capability can diverge directly within the same study.

## 2｜NEWLY DISCOVERED — Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning

**Authors:** Hamsa Bastani et al. · *PNAS* · 2025 · randomized field experiment, nearly 1,000 students  
**Radars:** `R1 R4` · **Evidence: [A]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122

AI improved practice performance, but students using unconstrained GPT Base later performed worse without AI than students who had never received AI access; tutor guardrails largely mitigated the effect.

**Why it matters:** preserving independent capability requires deliberate interaction design.

## 3｜NEWLY DISCOVERED — Partnering with Generative AI

**Authors:** Sebastian Maier, Manuel Schneider, Stefan Feuerriegel · CHI 2026 · RCT N=486 + replication N=640  
**Radars:** `R3 R4 R5 R6` · **Evidence: [A]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791185

Model-led rewriting improved idea quality but reduced diversity and perceived ownership. Reflective human-led modes also improved quality while better preserving diversity and ownership.

**Why it matters:** Control is becoming an experimentally manipulable design variable.

## 4｜NEWLY DISCOVERED — Relying on AI at work reduces self-efficacy, ownership, and meaning

**Authors:** E. Lee, Y. Yin, Nan Jia, Cheryl Wakslak · *Scientific Reports* · 2026-03-15 · preregistered experiment N=269 + survey N=270  
**Radars:** `R1 R4 R6` · **Evidence: [A]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-42312-6

Passive copy-based AI use reduced self-efficacy, psychological ownership, and work meaningfulness; draft-first active collaboration largely preserved these outcomes. The study does not establish long-term skill loss.

## 5｜NEWLY DISCOVERED — Expertise Elevates AI Usage

**Authors:** Thomas F. Eisenmann et al. · *International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction* · online 2026-05-18 · preregistered experiment  
**Radars:** `R1 R3 R5` · **Evidence: [B]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/10447318.2026.2669041

Professional artists retained advantages in replication accuracy and divergent ideas when using text-to-image AI, showing that domain expertise can transfer into AI-mediated tasks.

## 6｜NEWLY DISCOVERED — Understanding Critical Thinking in Generative AI Use

**Authors:** G. Lau et al. · *Computers in Human Behavior Reports* · 2026 · six studies, N=1,341  
**Radars:** `R2 R3 R6` · **Evidence: [B]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbr.2026.101103

A 13-item measure operationalizes critical AI use through **Verification, Motivation, and Reflection**. Higher scores predicted richer verification behavior, better veracity judgment, and deeper responsibility reflection. This narrows rather than closes the agency-measurement gap.

## 7｜NEWLY DISCOVERED — Whose Code Is It?

**Authors:** J. Seo, Elmira Deldari, Helena M. Mentis · IUI 2026 · within-subject experiment  
**Radars:** `R3 R6` · **Evidence: [B]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3742413.3789121

Higher coding-assistant autonomy shifted possession, identity, and responsibility attribution away from the human, while disclosure and accountability intentions remained more stable.

**Why it matters:** ownership and responsibility should not be collapsed into one construct.

## 8｜NEW — Identical errors, different responsibilities

**Authors:** Dimas Cassimiro Nascimento, Daliton da Silva · *AI and Ethics* · **2026-08-26**  
**Radars:** `R2 R6` · **Evidence: [C]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-026-01330-w

The paper argues that identical factual errors can involve different epistemic responsibility depending on whether verification was competent, proportionate, and sufficiently independent. Disclosure alone is not a complete governance model.

## 9｜NEWLY DISCOVERED — Human-AI Collaboration in Healthcare

**Authors:** Joshua Strong et al. · *npj Digital Medicine* · 2026-06-20 · scoping review, 140 empirical studies  
**Radars:** `R5 R6` · **Evidence: [B]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-026-02918-6

Benefits depended on task fit, workflow integration, training, and calibrated trust. Most evaluation still relies on short-term task metrics rather than patient/system outcomes.

## 10｜NEWLY DISCOVERED — Human-AI Synergy Supports Collective Creative Search

**Authors:** Chenyi Li et al. · arXiv:2602.10001 · 2026  
**Radars:** `R3 R5 R8` · **Evidence: [B]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.10001

Hybrid human–AI groups achieved the highest performance while preserving high outcome diversity in a controlled collective-search task.

**Why it matters:** AI does not inevitably compress variance; group composition and interaction architecture matter.

## 11｜NEWLY DISCOVERED — Learning social norms enhances compatibility in dynamic human-AI coordination

**Authors:** Yi Yang et al. · arXiv:2607.07021 · 2026-07-08  
**Radars:** `R5 R9` · **Evidence: [B]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.07021

Norm principles extracted from 3,456 human interactions improved closed-loop human–AI coordination when encoded into an AI agent. This remains a controlled-task preprint.

## 12｜NEWLY DISCOVERED — Humans learn to prefer trustworthy AI over human partners

**Authors:** Yaomin Jiang et al. · arXiv:2507.13524 · 2025 · three experiments, N=975  
**Radars:** `R8 R9` · **Evidence: [B]** · DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.13524

In mixed human/bot mini-societies, AI identity disclosure initially reduced partner selection but enabled learning about partner-type behavior; reliable AI could gradually outcompete humans for partnership.

# Evidence convergence

### 1. From AI exposure to interaction architecture

Across education, work, and creative co-production, human-led, bounded, draft-first, reflective, or verification-rich workflows more often preserve independent performance, ownership, or diversity than passive/model-led/open delegation. This does **not** establish one universal best workflow, but it gives **Control** a stronger empirical basis.

### 2. Retention is becoming measurable

The literature is starting to use AI-on → AI-off tests, supervised no-AI transfer, return-to-manual-work outcomes, and independent revision. **Retention** is now entering experimental design, though evidence remains short-term and task-specific.

### 3. Expertise can transfer into AI-mediated work

The artist experiment and healthcare synthesis both argue against a simple leveling narrative. Domain models, evaluation skill, error detection, task fit, and calibrated trust remain consequential.

### 4. Variance requires a conditional model

Model-led co-creation can reduce diversity, while hybrid collective search can preserve it. The defensible claim is that **AI changes population search dynamics; direction depends on interaction structure, model heterogeneity, group composition, and transmission.**

# Conclusions not supported

- The evidence does not establish generalized long-term cognitive decline from frequent AI use.
- Short-term no-AI transfer effects cannot be extrapolated into years of professional deskilling.
- Human-led interaction is not always superior on immediate output quality.
- AI does not inevitably homogenize populations.
- Psychological ownership is not equivalent to legal authorship or ultimate accountability.

# Frontier signals

**Tools for Thought [S]:** Microsoft Research / CHI is moving from field mapping toward operational interaction frameworks and implementable tools. `R4 R5 R6`

**Rahwan / CHM / Machine Culture [S]:** no new seven-day R7 result displaced the 2026-08-18 *Nature Communications* transmission experiment as the current anchor. The frontier is extending toward collective norm formation, hybrid partner selection, social learning, and mixed societies. `R7 R8 R9`

# Priority gaps

1. Months/years-long AI-on / AI-off retention.
2. Expert longitudinal trajectories.
3. Decomposition of bounded-support mechanisms.
4. Behavioral validation of agency measures.
5. Network designs that preserve collective variance while improving performance.
6. Real-world persistence of Machine Culture feedback loops.
