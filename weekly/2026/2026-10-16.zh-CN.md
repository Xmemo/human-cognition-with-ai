## 核实证据

**1. What task to offload to GenAI in the writing feedback process? – Effects of task-offloading approaches on EFL learners’ writing skill development**

**NEW [B] R1 R2 R3 R4** · Lai 等 · *Computers & Education* · 2026 年 10 月（日期未详）· N=101 · DOI 10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105675。AI 起草、学生评价修改的写作收益最大；准实验未拆分机制。

**2. The impact of artificial intelligence on students’ metacognition: a systematic review**

**NEW [B] R2 R4** · Ji、Tan · *Frontiers in Psychology* · 2026-10-08 · 22 项研究 · DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1947198。引导式与直接答案式交互有差异；研究异质，不能确定因果优劣。

**3. The impact of human-AI co-creation on perceived creativity in generative AI art: a serial mediation mechanism of psychological ownership, perceived agency, and creative flow**

**NEW [B] R3 R5 R6** · Wang 等 · *Frontiers in Psychology* · 2026-10-09 · N=600 · DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1986050。提高的是主观创造性感受，非客观能力。

**4. Generative AI in creative education: baseline-linked evidence on learning processes, artistic performance, and authorship**

**NEW [B] R1 R3 R6** · Shen 等 · *Frontiers in Psychology* · 2026-10-02 · N=133 · DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1956939。感知支持提高，艺术成绩未同步提高；仅观察性关联。

**5. College students' metacognitive awareness of generative-AI reliance: an experimental study of decision confidence and attribution bias**

**NEWLY DISCOVERED [B] R2 R4 R5** · Ren · *Frontiers in Psychology* · 2026-09-15 · N=342 · DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1926110。反思降低错误建议采纳（62.4%→39.7%）；未测长期结果。Consensus 已 fetch。

**6. Designing Against Deskilling: Metacognitive Feedback Reduces Cognitive Offloading to LLM Assistants**

**NEWLY DISCOVERED [B] R1 R2 R4** · Maier 等 · *arXiv* · 2026-09-17 · N=704 · DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2609.20143。反馈改善无 AI 算术测试，奖励无显著效果；短期预印本。

**7. Your Programming Students' Cognition with ChatGPT: Higher Performance, Lower Retention, and Reduced Ownership**

**NEWLY DISCOVERED [B] R1 R3 R6** · Bergh 等 · *arXiv* · 2026-09-18 · N=55 · DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2609.21194。代码表现更好，回忆更低；但遗忘速度无显著差异。小样本预印本。

**8. Self-reported learning-oriented GenAI use under perceived teacher-referenced normative tension in Chinese higher education**

**NEW [B] R2 R9** · Yang 等 · *Frontiers in Psychology* · 2026-10-02 · N=1004 · DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969743。规范冲突感与自报学习使用正相关；方向不明。

**9. Collective creativity in hybrid societies**

**NEWLY DISCOVERED [C] R3 R7 R8** · Youngblood 等 · *arXiv* · 2026-09-02 · DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2609.02620。区分作品新颖性与群体多样性；概念性综合。
