## Selected primary-verified items

**1. What task to offload to GenAI in the writing feedback process? – Effects of task-offloading approaches on EFL learners’ writing skill development**

**NEW [B] R1 R2 R3 R4** · Lai, Pan, Guo, Cui · *Computers & Education* · October 2026 (day unknown) · N=101 quasi-experiment · DOI 10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105675. AI drafting plus learner evaluation/revision had strongest writing gains. **Limit:** bundled EFL treatments, no long-run test.

**2. The impact of artificial intelligence on students’ metacognition: a systematic review**

**NEW [B] R2 R4** · Ji, Tan · *Frontiers in Psychology* · 2026-10-08 · 22 studies · DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1947198. Guided and direct-answer interaction patterns differ. **Limit:** heterogeneous evidence, no causal ranking.

**3. The impact of human-AI co-creation on perceived creativity in generative AI art: a serial mediation mechanism of psychological ownership, perceived agency, and creative flow**

**NEW [B] R3 R5 R6** · Wang, Chen, Yang · *Frontiers in Psychology* · 2026-10-09 · two experiments N=600 · DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1986050. Co-creation raised perceived creativity; moderate AI involvement scored highest. **Limit:** subjective ratings, not independent creative skill.

**4. Generative AI in creative education: baseline-linked evidence on learning processes, artistic performance, and authorship**

**NEW [B] R1 R3 R6** · Shen, Lee, He · *Frontiers in Psychology* · 2026-10-02 · observational N=133 · DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1956939. Perceived support rose without assessed art gains; authorship ratings were lower with content generation. **Limit:** association, not causation.

**5. College students' metacognitive awareness of generative-AI reliance: an experimental study of decision confidence and attribution bias**

**NEWLY DISCOVERED [B] R2 R4 R5** · Ren · *Frontiers in Psychology* · 2026-09-15 · experiment N=342 · DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1926110. Reflection lowered incorrect-advice acceptance 62.4%→39.7%. **Limit:** immediate task; Consensus ID 12531827b8065d769185562e78e257ee fetched and publisher-verified.

**6. Designing Against Deskilling: Metacognitive Feedback Reduces Cognitive Offloading to LLM Assistants**

**NEWLY DISCOVERED [B] R1 R2 R4** · Maier et al. · *arXiv* · 2026-09-17 · preregistered N=704 · DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2609.20143. Feedback reduced offloading (OR=.47) and improved unaided arithmetic (OR=1.51); reward null. **Limit:** preprint, short task.

**7. Your Programming Students' Cognition with ChatGPT: Higher Performance, Lower Retention, and Reduced Ownership**

**NEWLY DISCOVERED [B] R1 R3 R6** · Bergh et al. · *arXiv* · 2026-09-18 · N=55 analyzed · DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2609.21194. Coding 89% vs 69%; immediate recall 41% vs 53%; 48h 39% vs 52%. **Counterfinding:** forgetting rate not significantly different. **Limit:** small preprint.

**8. Self-reported learning-oriented GenAI use under perceived teacher-referenced normative tension in Chinese higher education**

**NEW [B] R2 R9** · Yang, Yang, Liu · *Frontiers in Psychology* · 2026-10-02 · survey N=1004 · DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969743. Norm tension positively associated with self-reported learning use. **Limit:** reverse direction possible; no observed cultural transmission.

**9. Collective creativity in hybrid societies**

**NEWLY DISCOVERED [C] R3 R7 R8** · Youngblood et al. · *arXiv* · 2026-09-02 · DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2609.02620. Conceptually separates artifact novelty and population diversity. **Limit:** not new group-level experimental evidence.

## Synthesis and contradictions

Task-stage evaluation, reflection and feedback are candidate control mechanisms, not universal recipes. Higher assisted performance need not entail better recall, assessed creativity or authorship. R7–R9 have no new long-run causal result.
