# Iyad Rahwan — Frontier Watch

**Role in this observatory:** long-term priority researcher for the transition from individual human–AI interaction to machine behaviour, machine culture, collective cognition, and cultural evolution.

Iyad Rahwan is Director of the **Center for Humans and Machines** at the Max Planck Institute for Human Development. His official research interests include computational social science, the social and economic impact of AI, human psychology and intelligent machines, human cooperation, human–machine cooperation, and AI ethics/governance. See the [official Max Planck profile](https://www.mpib-berlin.mpg.de/staff/iyad-rahwan) and [publication list](https://www.mpib-berlin.mpg.de/staff/iyad-rahwan/publications).

## Why he is a standing watch

Rahwan's work provides a bridge across several of this project's radars:

- **Machine behaviour:** treating intelligent machines as behavioural actors embedded in social systems.
- **Machine culture:** asking whether machine-generated innovations can enter, persist in, and reshape human culture.
- **Human–AI groups:** cooperation, delegation, trust, and mixed human/machine systems.
- **Cumulative cultural evolution:** variation, social learning, mediation, transmission, and preservation.
- **Expertise with AI:** whether domain expertise continues to improve AI-mediated performance.
- **AI metacognition / wisdom:** what it would mean for machines to represent their own limits, uncertainty, and context appropriately.
- **Algorithmic mediation:** how recommendation and AI systems alter exposure, language, behaviour, and collective dynamics.

## Weekly watch protocol

Every weekly run checks the official Max Planck publication/project pages plus canonical sources for:

1. new peer-reviewed papers and accepted/in-press work;
2. new preprints;
3. Machine Culture / Center for Humans and Machines project updates;
4. talks or research agendas that reveal a genuine direction change;
5. close collaborators when the work directly touches the 3×3 radar map.

**Priority does not mean quota.** A weekly brief should include Rahwan/CHM only when there is a qualifying new or newly relevant signal.
